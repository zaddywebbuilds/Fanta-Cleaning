The Move Out Cleaning Checklist Landlords Actually Inspect

The rooms and details a walkthrough stops at, in the order an inspection usually works through them.

22 September 2026 · 6 min read

An empty unit is the hardest thing to clean, because nothing is left to hide behind. Inspections are also predictable: they look at the same places in roughly the same order, every time. Work the list in that order and you stop guessing.

The kitchen, which decides most of it

  • Inside the oven. The first thing checked and the most commonly failed. Racks out, interior degreased, door glass clear.
  • Behind and under the appliances. Pull the fridge and the stove where they move. Both hide a year of crumbs and grease.
  • Every cupboard and drawer, inside. Emptied, vacuumed, wiped, including the tops of the upper cabinets.
  • The sink and taps. Limescale off, drain clear, no standing residue in the overflow.

The bathrooms, which decide the rest

  • Grout and silicone. Scrubbed rather than mopped over. Discoloured silicone sometimes needs replacing, not cleaning, and that is worth knowing before the inspection.
  • Shower glass. Soap film and limescale removed so it is genuinely clear in the light.
  • Behind and around the toilet base. Consistently missed, consistently checked.
  • The extractor vent cover. Dusty vents read as neglect even when the rest is spotless.

Everywhere else

  • Skirting and door frames. Wiped by hand, including the tops.
  • Closets and shelving, inside. Including the rail and the top shelf nobody reaches.
  • Switch plates and handles. Where fingerprints accumulate for years.
  • Window tracks and sills. Vacuumed first, then wiped, or you just smear it.
  • Floors, wall to wall. Now that the furniture is gone, mop to the edges rather than the middle.

The things people forget

Light fittings and the inside of shades. The balcony door track. The inside of the washing machine drawer and seal. The top of the fridge. The radiator fins. None of them take long, and all of them are visible to someone looking for a reason.

Timing it properly

Clean after the movers, not before. A half empty unit has to be cleaned twice, and you will pay for both. If there is a handover time, say so when booking so the day is planned around it.

Finish with photos of every room. They settle most deposit disputes before they start, and they cost nothing to take.
